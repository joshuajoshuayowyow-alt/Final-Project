01 / GET TO KNOW THE TOPIC

What is
social media?

Social media has become an important part of everyday life. Platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and messaging applications allow people to communicate, learn, share experiences, and discover information.

However, social media can also contribute to misinformation, cyberbullying, privacy problems, and excessive screen time when it is used without care. This project encourages safe, balanced, and responsible online behavior.

04 / BE AWARE

Risks to look out for

Think before you click, post, share, or respond.

!

Cyberbullying

Hurtful messages, harassment, or embarrassing posts directed at another person.

?

Misinformation

False or misleading information that spreads when people share without checking facts.

⌁

Scams and suspicious links

Messages or websites designed to trick users into giving away money or account details.

◈

Privacy problems

Personal information, photos, or location details may be exposed or shared without permission.

◷

Excessive screen time

Too much time online can affect sleep, school responsibilities, and in-person relationships.

05 / PROTECT YOURSELF

Practice online safety

Small habits can make your online experience safer and more positive.

01

Use strong passwords. Create unique passwords and never share them with other people.

02

Think before posting. Avoid sharing private details, passwords, or live location publicly.

03

Check before sharing. Verify information using reliable sources before reposting it.

04

Be respectful. Communicate kindly and report bullying or harmful content.

05

Set healthy limits. Take breaks and make time for school, rest, and offline activities.
